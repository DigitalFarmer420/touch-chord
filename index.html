Touch Chord

Welcome to Touch Chord. Every song that ever stayed with you, the one from a summer night or a long drive home, began with a few chords that belonged together. Touch Chord is your chord progression generator. You have a family of chords at your fingertips, ones that have always known how to harmonize with each other.

Thank you for being here. May you find your expression in music, and create something amazing.

  1. 1. Play chord progressions

    1. Select key. Swipe the note to choose your song’s home.
    2. Choose Major or Minor. Major sounds happy, and Minor sounds sad.
    3. Pick a chord progression. Choose a set of chords that belong together.
    4. Make a chord yourself.

    * The numbers on the chords show the order to play them in the progression.

  2. 2. Sustain

    Play a chord 1, then hold Sustain 2, and the notes keep ringing. Let go, and they gently fade away.

  3. 3. Style

    Tap a style. It changes the sound and the chords.

  4. 4. Understand the suggestion grid

    The chords on the right are picked just for you, and every one sounds good in your key and style. Happy chords sit above the smiley face, and sad ones sit below the frown. Tap any chord to play it, and the grid shows you what could come next. Swipe the key left to go lower, or right to go higher.

  5. 5. Make a chord Full mode

    Want to make your own chord? For A major 7, hold Major 7 in the chord table. Then press A and hear it come alive. Try any shape on any note, and explore.

  6. 6. Full Range Full mode

    Full Range enables all keys to be available.
