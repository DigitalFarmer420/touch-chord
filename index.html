Touch Chord

When on, tap the marked next chord, or in Full mode its root key in any octave, and the song moves on like Next. In Kids, tap the gold pad or the gold key in any octave. Not during Play. When off, taps only play sound.

Help

Welcome to Touch Chord. Every song that ever stayed with you, the one from a summer night or a long drive home, began with a few chords that belonged together. Touch Chord is your chord progression generator. You have a family of chords at your fingertips, ones that have always known how to harmonize with each other.

Thank you for being here. May you find your expression in music, and create something amazing.

Hold your phone either way. Upright, the chord grid is biggest and the piano sits below it. Sideways, the grid gets wider, and on Android the status bar hides to give it room: swipe down from the top edge to see it for a moment.

Settings (the mode button at the top right) shows the version and the full build name. Tap Copy next to the build to share it when you report a problem.

  1. 1. Play chord progressions

    1. Select key. Swipe the note to choose your song’s home.
    2. Choose Major or Minor. Major sounds happy, and Minor sounds sad.
    3. Pick a chord progression. Choose a set of chords that belong together.
    4. Make a chord yourself.

    * The numbers on the chords show the order to play them in the progression.

    Tap Next to play the chords one at a time, or Play to hear the whole progression. The chord that is playing grows a little, so you can follow along. In Expert, a small dot marks the chord that Play will sound next, and a yellow dot marks its root key on the piano.

    In Simple, the next chord has a gold outline. With Play along on in Settings (the mode button at the top right), tap that chord, or in Expert its root key, and it moves on just like Next. In Expert, the key is the button at the top left (C major ▾), and Next and ▶ are at the top right.

  2. 2. Sustain

    Play a chord 1, then hold Sustain 2, and the notes keep ringing. Let go, and they gently fade away. In Expert, it is the SUSTAIN button next to All notes.

  3. 3. Style

    Tap a style. It changes the sound and the chords. In Simple, the styles are the buttons along the top. In Expert, tap the style name 1 to pick one.

    Found a progression you like? In Expert, tap Lock 2 to keep it when you change the style. Tap it again to let the list change with the style.

  4. 4. Understand the suggestion grid

    The chords in the grid are picked just for you, and every one sounds good in your key and style. Happy chords sit above the row of notes , and sad ones sit below it . The faces are only a guide here in How to; they are no longer shown beside the grid, and they are not buttons. Tap any chord to play it, and the grid shows you what could come next. Swipe the key left to go lower, or right to go higher.

    In Expert, a chord with a solid orange edge, like B♭ here 2, is borrowed from a nearby key. It adds color and still sounds good in your song.

  5. 5. Play a chord from the piano Expert

    Tap a key on the piano 1 and you hear the chord that belongs to that note in your key. In C major, A plays A minor. Now 2 shows its name and its notes. Try every key, and explore.

  6. 6. All notes Expert

    Keys outside your key are softly faded 2. Their names stay easy to read, and they stay quiet. Tap All notes 1 to unlock every key and chord, even the ones outside your key. The line under it changes from “in key only” to “all 12”.

  7. 7. Keyboard Expert

    Tap the ↻ keys button 1 to switch your keys. It sits on the piano’s top bar when your phone is upright, and in the left column when it is sideways. Octave shows one octave that starts on your key, low notes on the left. Keyboard shows the whole two-octave keyboard. Buttons lays the notes out as a grid of buttons. Keys light up exactly as they sound, and the keyboard stays put while you tap. The app remembers your choice.

    To slide a finger across the keys, use Keyboard 2. In Octave, each touch plays just the key it starts on.

  8. 8. Band Expert

    Open Sound. Under Voicing, slide Band to the right to add bass and drums: 0 is off, 1 to 100 sets how loud. With your phone sideways, scroll the panel down to reach it. The app remembers your level.

  9. 9. Upload a song Expert

    Upload is in Expert. In Simple, tap the mode button at the top right and pick Expert 1. Then tap Song 2 to open My songs, with the form to upload a song.

    Type a Song title and the Artist 1. Leave the title empty to get up to 20 songs by the artist. Tap Copy for AI 2 and paste it into any AI. Paste its answer in the box 3 and tap Load 4. The result shows right under Load.

    Your songs are listed under My songs. Tap a song to play it. Tap the pencil to see its chords and words, or the trash can to remove it.

    When a song has words, two lines show above the chords: the line you are singing, with the word being sung lit up 1, and the next line in a lighter color below it 2. They roll up as the song moves on, and a long line slides along so the sung word stays in view. On an iPad you see three lines. A part of the song without words shows its chords there instead. The word where the next chord change lands is underlined in yellow, with that chord’s name above it in the same colour as on the grid (beside it when the phone is sideways, and on an iPad), and it glows about a beat before the change. Simple shows a song’s words the same way, three lines at a time (two sideways or on a small phone), with the chord’s name beside the word. Tap Lock 3 to lock or unlock the song’s chords.

  10. 10. Kids

    Pick Kids in Settings (the mode button at the top right) for big, friendly chords and sing-along songs: pick a song from the list. Seven big pads play a happy (major) chord on each note of the key. The gold pad 2 and the gold key 3 follow the tune: tap either one, or that key in any octave, to play the song’s next note, and the words show which one you are singing. A letter above a word, in that pad’s colour, shows the pad that comes next when the pad changes. This needs Play along on in Settings. Tap the house to go back to the songs. Grown-ups: press and hold Kids 1 to open Settings.
