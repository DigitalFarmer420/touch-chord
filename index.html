Touch Chord

About Touch Chord

Touch Chord turns one finger into a full chord.
It's for anyone who wants to play chords without reading music.
A chord is 3 or more notes played at once. Chords that sound good together make up a key.

  • Learn chords: try a random chord and see its name and notes.
  • Sing along: play chords under your voice.
  • Beginner to making music: start with one chord, then follow the suggestions into a song.

How-to

1. Make a chord

Hold maj7 (1), tap A (2) = Amaj7.

2. Chord Suggestion

Tap Key (1), pick A, close. Then tap a tile (2) to hear the next chord.

3. Sustain

Play (1), hold Sustain (2): notes ring and fade. Let go to stop.

4. Style

Tap a style (1). It changes the sound and suggestions.

5. Full Range

Gray keys are outside your key. Tap Full Range (1) to play them all.

Sound and FX live in the gear's Advanced settings.
