Touch Chord

How to

Make a chord

  1. Hold a chord button on the left.
  2. Tap a key.
  3. The big letter shows your chord, so holding maj7 and tapping A gives you Amaj7.

Next

Your first chord sets the key. Tap the Key button under Now to pick a different one.

Keys outside your key are grayed out. Turn on All keys to play every key.

  • The row under the key shows the 7 chords in your key, and the one you're playing is lit.
  • The tiles are good chords to play next. Tap one to hear it.
  • The number (I, IV, V…) is where that chord sits in the key, and I is home.
  • The short word says how it feels, like “leans home”, “sad” or “tension”.
  • A dashed tile is borrowed from outside the key, so it sounds spicier.
  • Bright, Dark, Tension and Color are the mood of each column.
  • Tap ▼ to show more.

Sustain

Hold Sustain to keep the notes ringing, and let go to stop.
