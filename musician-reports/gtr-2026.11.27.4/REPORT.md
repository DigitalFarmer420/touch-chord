# Guitar shapes check, dev 2026.11.27.4 (deploy 33cb801, develop 20df938)
Method: node harness cmp.mjs calls the app's js/instruments/resolve.js resolveVoicings("guitar") for 40 qualities x 12 roots (480) and checks the first voicing with my shapes.json checker (tones, root/bass lowest), and compares with my pick. Not checked: UI rendering, diagrams, root dots, strum audio, slash chords, the 315-chord app inventory.
Results (cmp-output.txt): 480 resolve, 0 empty, 0 simplified; 458 correct, 22 wrong bass; first voicing == my pick 75/480; my pick anywhere in the list 126/480; 373 first voicings come from the generator; 260 have muted inner strings (inner-muted.txt).
N26 (major): open library matches by note set, not bass. 22 chords show another chord's grip with a non-root bass: C6 = Am grip x02210, G6 = Em 022000, F6 xx0211, Csus2 3x0013, Dsus2 x02230, Asus2 022200, D7sus4 3x0013, E7sus4 x02230, B7sus4 022200, D9sus x32030, A9sus 320203, 6sus4 and sus4add9 cases. Lowest sounding note must be the root (or slash bass).
N27 (major, playability): the app library has 10 movable templates (E/A for maj, m, 7, m7, maj7); shapes.json has 64 open + 95 movable (E/A/D) for 40 types. Everything else is generated, often with inner muted strings or jumps (Cdim7 x3x242, Dbdim7 9x8x80, Ebaug7 (11)x(11)00x, Abmmaj7 4xx00x), which can't be strummed. Suggest loading shapes.json's openShapes + movableTemplates and keeping the generator as last resort.
