Touch Chord

When on, tap the marked next chord, or in Full mode its root key in any octave, and the song moves on like Next. In Kids, tap the gold pad or the gold key in any octave. Not during Play. When off, taps only play sound.

Help

Welcome to Touch Chord. Every song that ever stayed with you, the one from a summer night or a long drive home, began with a few chords that belonged together. Touch Chord is your chord progression generator. You have a family of chords at your fingertips, ones that have always known how to harmonize with each other.

Thank you for being here. May you find your expression in music, and create something amazing.

  1. 1. Play chord progressions

    1. Select key. Swipe the note to choose your song’s home.
    2. Choose Major or Minor. Major sounds happy, and Minor sounds sad.
    3. Pick a chord progression. Choose a set of chords that belong together.
    4. Make a chord yourself.

    * The numbers on the chords show the order to play them in the progression.

    Tap Next to play the chords one at a time, or Play to hear the whole progression. The chord that is playing grows a little, so you can follow along. In Full mode, a dashed outline gently marks the chord that Play will sound next.

    The next chord has a gold outline. With Play along on in the gear menu, tap that chord, or in Full mode its root key, and it moves on just like Next.

  2. 2. Sustain

    Play a chord 1, then hold Sustain 2, and the notes keep ringing. Let go, and they gently fade away.

  3. 3. Style

    Tap a style. It changes the sound and the chords.

  4. 4. Understand the suggestion grid

    The chords in the grid are picked just for you, and every one sounds good in your key and style. Happy chords sit above the row of notes , and sad ones sit below it . The faces are only a guide here in How to; they are no longer shown beside the grid, and they are not buttons. Tap any chord to play it, and the grid shows you what could come next. Swipe the key left to go lower, or right to go higher.

  5. 5. Make a chord Full mode

    Want to make your own chord? For A major 7, hold Major 7 in the chord table. Then press A and hear it come alive. Try any shape on any note, and explore.

  6. 6. Full Range Full mode

    Tap Full Range to unlock every key and chord, even the ones outside your key.

  7. 7. Keyboard Full mode

    Open Layout to choose your keys. Octave shows one octave that starts on your key, low notes on the left. Full shows the whole two-octave keyboard. Keys light up exactly as they sound, and the keyboard stays put while you tap. The app remembers your choice.

  8. 8. Band Full mode

    Open Sound. Under Voicing, slide Band to the right to add bass and drums: 0 is off, 1 to 100 sets how loud. The app remembers your level.

  9. 9. Upload a song Full mode

    Upload is in Full mode. If the Simple switch at the top is on, tap it to turn it off 1. Then tap the Upload button 2: the small square button with an arrow pointing up, on the left side of the screen. On a phone it sits just right of Full Range, under Play. On a tablet or computer it sits just right of Play.

    Type a Song title and the Artist 1. Leave the title empty to get up to 20 songs by the artist. Tap Copy for AI 2 and paste it into any AI. Paste its answer in the box 3 and tap Load 4. The result shows right under Load.

    Your songs are listed under My songs. Tap a song to play it. Tap the pencil to see its chords and words, or the trash can to remove it.

    When a song has words, two lines show above the chords: the line you are singing, and the next one. They scroll up as the song moves on.

  10. 10. Kids

    Tap Kids for big, friendly chords and sing-along songs: Twinkle, Mary’s Lamb, Row Your Boat, Hot Cross Buns, Old MacDonald, Itsy Bitsy Spider, London Bridge, Hey Diddle Diddle, Head, Shoulders, Knees and Toes, Baa, Baa, Black Sheep and Hickory Dickory Dock. Seven big pads play a happy (major) chord on each note of the key. The gold pad and the gold key follow the tune: tap either one, or that key in any octave, to play the next note, and the words show which one you are singing. This needs Play along on in the gear menu.
